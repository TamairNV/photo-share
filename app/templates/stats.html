How these are counted
  • People: each phone or computer gets a random id the first time it opens a gallery, so people on the same Wi-Fi count separately. The same person on two devices, or after clearing their browser, counts twice. Visits from before this change were counted by internet address instead.
  • You are left out of everything except Server data sent and your own uploads. That works on any device where you've opened this admin area at least once, including anything it did before then. Open /admin once on your phone if you check the gallery there.
  • Link previews (the card WhatsApp or iMessage shows when someone pastes the link) and bots aren't people. They show up only under Devices.
  • Photos looked at: opening a photo full size. One person flicking back to the same photo counts once. Scrolling past thumbnails isn't counted.
  • Taken home: files that left the server onto someone's device: Save to Photos (counted once the phone's share sheet is done, even if they picked AirDrop or Messages instead), the Original download button, and ZIPs. A ZIP only counts once it has been sent in full, so a cancelled Download all doesn't count as every photo. Saving the same photo twice counts twice.
  • Uploaded by guests: files sent through upload links. Your own uploads in admin are listed separately, and files you copy onto the drive directly aren't counted at all.
  • Problems: server errors are things that broke and are worth a look. Not found is mostly bots trying addresses that don't exist, or old links, and is usually fine.
  • Stats are kept for 120 days.
